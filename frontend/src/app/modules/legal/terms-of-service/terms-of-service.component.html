4. Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refund Policy

We understand that travel plans may change unexpectedly. Our cancellation guidelines are structured as follows:

  • More than 24 Hours Notice: 100% full refund of the advance token, or complimentary date rescheduling without penalty.
  • Between 12 to 24 Hours Notice: 50% refund of advance token, or 100% transfer towards any future journey valid for up to 6 months.
  • Less than 12 Hours or Vehicle Dispatched: Advance token is non-refundable to cover driver mobilization and garage dispatch costs.
  • Refund Processing: Approved refunds are credited back to the customer's bank account or UPI mode within 3 to 5 business days.
