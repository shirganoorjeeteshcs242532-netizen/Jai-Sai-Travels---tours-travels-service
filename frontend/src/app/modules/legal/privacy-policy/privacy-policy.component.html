1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Jai Sai Travels, your privacy is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines our transparent practices regarding the collection, handling, storage, and protection of information gathered through our website, phone booking lines, WhatsApp dispatch desks, and in-person reservations.

We treat all personal passenger details with the utmost confidentiality and adhere to applicable data protection guidelines and privacy best practices.

3. How Your Information is Used

  • Booking Fulfillment: Allocating the vehicle (e.g. Toyota Innova Crysta), coordinating with designated chauffeurs, and sharing driver contact cards prior to pickup.
  • Flight Monitoring: Tracking delayed flights for airport pickups to adjust chauffeur reporting times without inconvenience to passengers.
  • Customer Support & Dispatch: Answering queries, accommodating emergency itinerary changes, and providing real-time trip coordination.
  • Invoicing & GST Billing: Generating transparent bills and corporate expense receipts upon trip completion.

4. Strict Zero Data Selling Guarantee

We strictly pledge that Jai Sai Travels never sells, rents, leases, or trades your personal contact information or trip history to third-party telemarketers, brokers, or external advertising companies.

Assigned drivers are only provided with the passenger's name, pickup landmark, and direct mobile number solely for the purpose of meeting and completing the scheduled journey.
